# 12.3 · Keep-style note modal layout and the "More" menu

**Goal:** Lay out the open-note modal like Google Keep: pin at the top right, label chips and the "Edited" date at the end of the note, and a bottom toolbar. "Change labels", "Colors", "Version history" and "Delete note" move into a "More" menu, with Keep-style pickers for labels and colors. Behavior stays the same.

**Spec:** Flow §10 (pin, archive, color, labels; history entries for all but color), §9 (version history), §16 (move to trash, restore, delete forever), and the Phase 1 "UI" paragraph in §19. Tasks 11.1 and 12.1. The owner approved this after task 12. Schema: `notes`, `labels`, `note_labels`, `history_events`.

**Model:** Claude Sonnet 5.5.

**Prompt:**

```text
Read AGENTS.md, brief.md, sections 9, 10 and 16 and the Phase 1 "UI" paragraph of section 19 in docs/Tracepad-Full-Flow.md. Then read web/src/ui/NoteModal.tsx, web/src/features/editor/Editor.tsx and NotesHome.tsx, and web/src/features/labels/NoteMeta.tsx and colors.ts. Do task 12.2 first, if it isn't done, so the label sort exists.
Do task 12.3. The owner approved this layout, modeled on Google Keep's open note (screenshots: a long note "Practise"):
- Size: about 600px wide, horizontally centered, starting about 48px from the top. Its height fits the content up to the viewport minus about 96px. The title, body, chips and date scroll together inside the modal with a thin scrollbar on the right; the bottom toolbar stays fixed.
- Top: the title on the left; "Pin" or "Unpin" as text at the top right, on the title's line.
- End of the content: label chips (rounded, small) on the left, and "Edited <date>" on the right from the note's updated time: the time if it's today, "Nov 3" this year, "Apr 29, 2024" otherwise.
- Bottom toolbar, all text and no icons for now: "Archive" or "Unarchive", then "More", with "Close" on the far right.
- "More" opens a small menu just above it: "Change labels", "Colors", "Version history", "Delete note".
  - "Change labels" opens a "Label note" panel in the same spot: an "Enter label name" field that filters, a scrollable checkbox list in the shared label order, and "Create "<typed name>"" when no label matches. Ticking or unticking adds or removes the label, with history entries as today.
  - "Colors" opens a panel in the same spot that shows the color swatches from colors.ts with their names as tooltips. It behaves as today: it changes the card and adds no history entry.
  - "Version history" opens /history/<note-uuid>, as today.
  - "Delete note" is today's move to trash, with the undo snackbar (section 16).
  - Clicking outside a menu or panel, or pressing Esc, closes only that menu or panel, not the note.
- A trashed note's toolbar shows "Restore" (from task 12.1) and "Delete forever" (which asks first), and no "More".
- Remove the old inline pin, archive, color and label controls in NoteMeta. Every action keeps its current data path and history behavior.
- Creating a note via "Take a note" stays inline and unchanged.
Keep the change scoped to the open-note modal.
Always write tests for the feature you're building, before or alongside the code. Then run ./tests/run-all.sh; every test must pass. Report.
```
