# 12.2 · "Edit labels" the Google Keep way

**Goal:** Manage labels the way Keep does. The side list shows the labels, then an "Edit labels" entry that opens a modal. The modal has "Create new label" on top and every label below it, sorted, with rename inline, delete on hover (after a confirmation), and a "Done" button. There's no create form in the side list.

**Spec:** Flow §10 (create, rename and delete labels; deleting removes the label from every note and keeps the notes), §6 (label views), and the Phase 1 "UI" paragraph in §19 ("UI matches Google Keep exactly"). Task 11.1 (modal behavior). The owner approved this after task 12. Schema: `labels`, `note_labels`.

**Model:** Claude Sonnet 5.5.

**Prompt:**

