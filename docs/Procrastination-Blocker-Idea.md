# Procrastination blocker

A future project seed. Not part of Tracepad's note-taking design.

When a reminder fires, the app adds friction instead of another notification you can swipe away. Until you do the task you committed to, even if that is only five or ten minutes, it blocks the distracting apps. A stricter mode blocks the whole phone. You can snooze and push the block later. You cannot skip it.

The idea comes from James Clear's Atomic Habits: make the good habit easy, and make the bad habit hard. A reminder alone is easy to ignore. A locked screen in front of the distracting app is not.

Total lockout has a real risk. People resent it, or they reboot the phone and walk around the block. A gentler version blocks only the distracting apps, and it makes you wait a minimum time before snooze is even available. The task still cannot be dismissed for good.
